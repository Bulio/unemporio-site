The story of this book

Cozy Cute Christmas Treats is the fourth book in our Christmas coloring series. After dinosaurs and woodland animals, the next subject came straight from the kitchen: gingerbread men, cookies, cocoa mugs, candy canes and cupcakes. It is the sweet-smelling corner of December, drawn for kids aged 4 to 8 and for adults who like a calm, easy page.

Inside there are eighty designs: a gingerbread man sitting in a cup, a snowman, a gingerbread house, a star cookie, a sleigh, cupcakes, a teapot, a cocoa mug with a scarf and plenty of candy canes. The shapes are big and simple, which makes it a good first book for small hands. Every page carries a short caption underneath.

The lines are thick, and every design is printed on one side only, so markers will not show through onto the next page. It suits a kitchen table in December: color a page while something bakes, or keep a few pages for the afternoons when the weather keeps everyone indoors.

It also works as a small Christmas present. The pages are square (8.5 x 8.5 inches), 162 pages in all, paperback. The drawings are created with the help of artificial intelligence, as stated on the Amazon listing.

Who it is for

For children aged 4 to 8 and for adults who like a calm, easy page. The shapes are big and simple, so the book is a good first one for small hands.
