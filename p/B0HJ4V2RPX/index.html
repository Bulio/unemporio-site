The story of this book

High on Colors is the second title in The IL TERZO DITO series (Bulio Editore), after the Italian-language Christmas title. No swear words this time: fifty-six psychedelic worlds to look at for a long while before you even start coloring. It is a book for adults who want to switch off.

Nothing is straight and nothing is symmetrical. Stairs end where they started, a face sits inside a bigger face, a planet hangs low enough to touch. Inside you will find floating astronauts, greenhouses, a van parked among ferns, a dancer spinning among sunflowers, lightning bolts and mushrooms. If you usually find mandalas too tidy, here the shapes bend and every page is different from the next.

The lines are thick and the shapes are big, so it colors well even with markers and you do not need fine pencils. The pages are printed on one side only and the back of every sheet is black, so marker bleed does not show through. It is slow coloring: one page in an evening, with no hurry to finish.

The book is also available in Italian (Fatti di Colori) and Spanish (Viaje de Colores). The pages are square (8.5 x 8.5 inches), 116 pages in all, paperback; adults only. The drawings are created with the help of artificial intelligence, as stated on the Amazon listing.

Who it is for

For adults who want to switch off. This is not a book for children. It suits people who like psychedelic art and slightly surreal images, and people who want a slow kind of coloring with no hurry.

It is also a fit for anyone who finds mandalas too tidy: here the shapes bend and every page is different from the next.
