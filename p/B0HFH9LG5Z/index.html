The story of this book

We wanted a Halloween coloring book that would not scare anybody. Halloween books usually lean on witches and skeletons and are made for grown-ups; here the stars are silly dinosaurs with friendly faces from the first page to the last. It is a book for kids aged 4 to 8, and also for the adult who sits down next to them with a marker.

Inside you will find eighty designs, one per page: dinosaurs in wizard hats, carved pumpkins, bubbling cauldrons, front doors to knock on for trick-or-treat and quiet autumn evenings in the garden. Every design has a short caption underneath, so there is something to read out loud as well as something to color.

The lines are thick and the open areas are big, so chunky crayons are enough and markers work very well. Each design is printed on one side only and the back of the page stays blank: ink will not show through onto the next picture, and a particularly good one can be taken out and put on the wall. One idea that works: one page each evening in the days before Halloween, or an afternoon snack with all the colors on the table.

The pages are square (8.5 x 8.5 inches), 162 pages in all, paperback. The drawings are created with the help of artificial intelligence, as stated on the Amazon listing.

Who it is for

For kids aged 4 to 8, who can stay inside thick outlines with chunky crayons. Adults will like it too: the lines are bold and the open areas are big, so it works well with markers and you can color next to a child.

It suits anyone looking for a cute Halloween rather than a spooky one. The faces stay friendly from the first page to the last.
