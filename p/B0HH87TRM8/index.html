The story of this book

After the Halloween dinosaurs and the Christmas dinosaurs we wanted a different subject for winter, and woodland animals in hats and scarves seemed the best fit for the soft mood of the holidays. Cozy Cute Woodland Friends is an easy coloring book for kids aged 4 to 8 and for adults who like a simple, relaxing drawing.

Inside there are eighty designs: a fox in a scarf, an owl inside a wreath, reindeer, hedgehogs with baubles, little bears, rabbits with presents, raccoons, mice and a log cabin with smoke from the chimney. The faces are sweet, the lines are thick and the open areas are big. Every page carries a short caption underneath.

The pages are printed on one side only and the back stays blank, so markers do not show through onto the next picture and you can use whatever you like, from crayons to broad-tip markers. A pleasant way to use it is one page each evening with the tree lights on, or an afternoon of coloring together.

It also makes a good Christmas present for a child who loves animals. The pages are square (8.5 x 8.5 inches), 162 pages in all, paperback. The drawings are created with the help of artificial intelligence, as stated on the Amazon listing.

Who it is for
