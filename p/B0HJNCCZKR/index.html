The story of this book

High on Music grew out of the drawings we made for the short videos of The IL TERZO DITO series (Bulio Editore). It seemed a shame to leave them on a screen, so we turned them into a coloring book of fifty plates. The theme is sound you can see.

Inside there are turntables that turn into spirals, speakers in bloom, guitars, notes and waves climbing to the edge of the page. The lines are dense, the areas big and there is no hurry: it is a book for adults who love music, records and psychedelic art, and who want a long project.

The pages are printed on one side only and the back of every sheet is black, so markers leave no bleed-through on the next page. The best way to use it is with the radio on, an hour in the evening, one plate at a time.

It can make a nice present for a seventies music fan. The book exists in three languages: Italian (Fatti di Musica), English (High on Music) and Spanish (Viaje de Música). The pages are square (8.5 x 8.5 inches), 104 pages in all, paperback; not for children. The drawings are created with the help of artificial intelligence, as stated on the Amazon listing.

Who it is for

For adults who love music, records and psychedelic art, and want a long project. Not for children.

A gift idea for a seventies music fan, or for anyone who likes an hour of coloring with the radio on.
